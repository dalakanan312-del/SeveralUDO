# Decades Tracker 4.6.50 — Lifetime allowance and Annual Baby Rolls

- Pregnancy-count rolls now establish one lifetime pregnancy allowance per Sim, separate from the yearly decision. Pregnancies count across all years; twins and larger births use one allowance.
- Optional Annual Baby Rolls use the supplied D20 age bands, all six modifiers, and the 1–10 success-range cap. Historical age is calculated at the start of the year and respects the save's year length. No natural annual roll applies from age 50 onward.
- Independent lifetime and annual automation switches, optional custom annual odds, eligibility restrictions, fertility bonuses, and established-partnership controls are available in Settings → Roll Automation.
- Today's roll cards explain the age calculation and modifiers. A success adds a have-a-baby-this-year task; actual pregnancy and birth records still drive the normal multiples, birth, infant-survival, and maternal checks.
- Current pregnancies, exhausted allowances, infertility, ineligible Sims, and births already recorded for the current year prevent additional annual attempts. Annual checks remain visible on Today for their year, without accumulating old attempts.
- Existing completed results remain unchanged. Redundant unfinished count rolls are retired, lifetime family plans remain consistent, and manual dismissals stay dismissed. Reopening a yearly result retracts its unfinished planning task.

Validated with 349 gameplay regression tests, two desktop-packaging checks, and desktop/mobile browser checks of the new controls. No database schema change or game-mod update is required. Existing saves and completed history are preserved; unrelated Sims 3 experiments are excluded.
