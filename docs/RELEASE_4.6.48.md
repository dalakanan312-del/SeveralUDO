# Decades Tracker 4.6.48 — Photo exits and clearer infant portraits

- Photos open in an in-app viewer with Close photo, Escape, fit/actual-size viewing, and Download. Closing preserves the page and scroll position.
- Persistent Back and Today controls are available across tracker pages, with native desktop controls for standalone images and error screens.
- Confirmation previews have a visible Close control that works even after a failed request. Closing does not confirm or decline a roll. An unfinished refresh cannot reopen a closed preview.
- Family-tree print views and online chronicle previews include return links. Downloaded chronicles remain self-contained.
- The optional ad prompt can be dismissed with Not now or Escape without consenting to ads or changing an existing preference.
- Infants are larger in decade snapshot compositions. Detached lower Boppy pillows are cropped conservatively; source photos and ordinary infant poses are preserved.
- Refresh saved portrait layout re-renders an existing album from its archived photos, without a new Tray scan. The original composite is retained.

Saved results, Sims, branches and the installed game mod are preserved. Unrelated Sims 3 experiments are not included.

Validation includes navigation, portrait, privacy, download, confirmation, and ad-consent regression tests, plus light/dark desktop/mobile browser checks and no-JavaScript navigation.
