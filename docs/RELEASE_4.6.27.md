# Decades Tracker 4.6.27

## Family Fortunes

Find the new minigames under **Play → Family Fortunes**:

- **Matchmaker’s Table:** negotiate a match with dowry parcels, property, heirlooms and specific promises. Each scenario has its own complication and three offer rounds.
- **Inheritance Dispute:** divide an estate among living beneficiaries, reserve funds and debts. Existing succession preferences and unpaid dowry promises are taken into account.

Drag cards, tap to select and place, or use the keyboard. Review the exact consequences before confirming. Confirmed endings record the choices in the storyline and create tracker plans and follow-up tasks; they do not directly move game money, marry Sims or kill Sims.

## Decline a roll and throw again

**Decline result** now discards the pending throw. Pressing **Roll** again generates a fresh result. Escape also declines the roll. A new throw can naturally land on the same number.

Declined throws remain in the audit history. Completed results stay unchanged. Refreshing a preview or retrying confirmation without declining keeps the original throw, and repeating an old decline cannot discard a newer throw.

## Compatibility

- Hosted synchronization accepts Family Fortunes records from the desktop tracker.
- Existing saves are preserved during an in-place desktop update.
- No game mod replacement is required for these features.
