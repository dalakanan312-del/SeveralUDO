# Infinite Decades play tools (4.6.42)

Open **Infinite Decades → Branch queue**. Nothing here changes which Sims are
loaded in the game, pauses game aging, or creates unplayed history.

1. **Branch queue:** one row per branch, with founders, split year, played-through
   date and status. Sort by oldest unfinished date, surname or your own order.
   The chosen sort stays in this browser; set custom positions in branch summaries.
2. **Resume summary:** choose Summary beside a branch for its last recorded
   events, pending-roll count, pregnancies, upcoming birthdays and handover notes.
3. **Household guard:** when a clock report identifies an active household that
   does not match the current branch, its complete report is held outside the
   branch timeline. A banner links to review. Accept an intentional household
   change or discard the report. Other branches are still excluded from imports.
   Household names/IDs must be available; absent evidence is not a match guarantee.
   At 100 pending reports, further reports stay in the relay until you review them.
4. **Switch preview:** Play shows outgoing and incoming tracker dates before
   confirmation. Tracker-calendar mode leaves the game clock unchanged.
   Separate-checkpoint mode still requires the appropriate game saves.
5. **Age checks:** summaries and switch previews compare recorded game stages
   with challenge-calendar stages, including scaled year lengths. Unknown birth
   dates and ordinary-aging-exempt occults are not compared. Warnings never edit
   a birthday; the game observation can be older than the tracker checkpoint.
6. **Play-until goals:** choose a year, next birth, next marriage or generation.
   A reached-goal banner links back to the oldest unfinished families. It does
   not finish a branch or switch it for you. Birth/marriage goals include new
   records on the same tracker day and exclude existing records.
7. **Historical corrections:** preview a name, birthplace, parent or legitimacy
   correction in every retained checkpoint copy. Completed rolls and birth dates
   stay unchanged. A backup and audit are retained. Undo restores only these
   fields, and refuses to overwrite later edits to them.
8. **Cross-branch transfers:** choose the receiving branch, the living Sims and
   any accompanying children. Optionally record the two partners' marriage.
   Both branches must first reach the same Global Day: transfers do not invent
   missing years. IDs, original parent links, portraits and completed rolls stay
   intact. Future children belong to the branch that records their birth.
9. **Same-year comparison:** Compare Branches accepts an end-of-year date.
   Unplayed years are unknown, not projected. Dated births, deaths and events can
   be compared; historical funds and occult states are not inferred from today's
   values. Missing death dates make historical survival unknown.
10. **Snapshot coverage:** Decade Snapshots shows which branches contributed,
    which have reached the year, who is not pictured and who has no stored
    portrait. A Tray scan may find photos not yet stored in the tracker.
11. **Suggested splits:** recent marriages and moves suggest living spouses and
    dependent children. Review and change the selected people before capturing.
    No family split happens automatically.
12. **Switch undo:** review a return to the previous branch. If reports or edits
    arrived, the preview says so. All new progress is preserved in the outgoing
    branch, never discarded as part of undoing the switch.

Previews are tied to the signed-in person, active save and branch, expire after
30 minutes, and cannot be confirmed twice. A changed affected record requires a
fresh review. Full dynasty exports include management history; held reports are
not published or sent to third parties by these tools.
