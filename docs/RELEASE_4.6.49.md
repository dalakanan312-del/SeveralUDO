# Decades Tracker 4.6.49 — Optional roll automation and everyday dice

- New **Settings → Roll Automation**, also linked from Today and Roll Tables, with seven independent category switches and individual source-rule overrides. New switches default off in existing saves.
- Optional annual pregnancy-allowance checks, HP witch-hunt and secrecy consequences, changeling suspicion, two mermaid dehydration survival checks, and confirmed vampire-feeding suspicion.
- Avatar and Game of Thrones scheduling uses known birth, age, household, year, outcome, and recorded prerequisite triggers. Confirm other situations once; annual sources then repeat while monitored. Unobservable voluntary actions are not inferred from vague game buffs. Dynamic tables require their actual source-defined step.
- Pausing an option retires unfinished generated work without changing completed history. Repeated reports do not duplicate checks, and manual dismissals stay dismissed. Master automation and frozen branches remain authoritative.
- Opt-in era-based annual marriage and remarriage tables, eligibility controls, successful-match handling, and the d6 refusal follow-up. Pending marriage, remarriage and refusal checks remain visible in Today's to-do view after their due day.
- New Quick Dice page for coins, standard dice, custom dice pools and modifiers. These practice/decision rolls do not alter Sims or scheduled results; recent throws are stored only in the browser.
- Roll-source statistics show readable categories instead of long internal identifiers; detailed source IDs are collapsed.
- Corrected the Crusades occurrence chain: the yearly event check and its conditional per-Sim survival checks use their own source-defined dice and outcomes. Completed history is not replayed.

Validation: 283 focused and regression tests, Quick Dice JavaScript checks, plus desktop/mobile browser checks of the new controls.

Existing Sims, branches, completed rolls and saves are retained. No database schema changes or new game mod is required. Unrelated Sims 3 experiments are excluded.
