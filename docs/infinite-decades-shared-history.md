# Infinite Decades: shared dynasty history (4.6.43)

Open **Infinite Decades → Dynasty History**. Your regular branch queue remains
compact; these ledgers are separate from the Sims game's data and from each
branch's rewindable checkpoint. They travel with a full save backup/export.

## Shared historical decisions

Choose an existing event, its Global Day, and the world-level outcome (for
example, the winning kingdom). Once recorded, other branches automatically see
it when they reach that day. Matching event rolls and Storyline show the shared
decision. The tracker never reuses or completes personal/household survival
rolls. An event may have one shared decision per dated occurrence.

## Contradictions

The Contradictions section compares retained birth dates, parent identities,
death dates, and marriage dates across branch checkpoints. It explains the
conflicting evidence without rewriting anything. Unknown dates are not treated
as proof of an error; a same-day marriage/death is not assumed contradictory.

## Spoiler-free history

Enable **Hide history later than the active branch’s date** in History & drama
preferences. The ledger, branch history, Timeline and Storyline hide future
dated events. Future death dates are removed from read-only historical
projections, not from stored records. Undated narrative is hidden because its
position cannot be verified. Management controls, full profiles, exports, and
the branch queue still expose the information needed to administer the save;
this is a reading preference, not an access-control or data-redaction system.

## Visits

Record the visitor, their home branch, receiving branch (the active family),
occasion and date. End the visit from the receiving branch when appropriate.
Visitors retain their owning branch and do not acquire host-branch aging,
pregnancy, or survival tasks. The tracker does not simulate unplayed years or
actually move Sims in the game. Optional drama prompts can include open visits.

## Pending inheritances and transfers

From the sending branch, choose **Pending handovers**. Departures use the current
Global Day; the receiving branch must not already be past it.

- **Family transfer:** select living members and leave someone living behind.
  Preview and confirm to reserve the selected people. They leave the active
  task list and wait in transit, retaining IDs, ancestry, birthdays, records,
  and portraits. The receiving checkpoint is not advanced or populated early.
- **Inheritance:** select the sending household and amount. A dated expense is
  recorded in the tracker economy ledger. Game-reported funds are unchanged.
- When the recipient is active at or after the departure date, a persistent
  banner links to **Review receipt**. Preview and confirm once. People retain
  their records and may have due tasks to review; inheritance creates one
  matching income entry for the selected receiving household. Reusing the
  confirmation cannot duplicate either.

No marriage is invented by a transfer. Record the marriage separately when it
occurs. Changed shared records or changed Sim ownership block receipt instead
of being overwritten. A branch can be parked while a handover waits for it.

## Parked branches

Park waiting or paused branches with a reason and an optional historical-year
reminder. Parked branches disappear from normal play suggestions but are not
completed or deleted. When the active calendar reaches the reminder year, a
banner appears. **Return to queue** makes the branch playable again. Switch away
from an active branch before parking it.

## Branch relationships and drama

Record Allied, Rival, Estranged, Feuding or Neutral with a date and notes. Enable
the optional drama preference to show contextual scenario prompts on the Drama
Deck. The latest relationship reached on the active date wins. Prompts are
story framing only: no source rule, die, game relationship, or outcome changes.

## Heirloom ownership

Start a chain from an existing tracked heirloom or a named new object. Choose
its owner and branch. Use **Preview next owner** to append later handovers. The
list retains every owner, date and note; spoiler-free mode shows only owners
known by the current date. This ledger does not physically move game objects.

## Decade checklist

Choose the snapshot/closing year. Each relevant branch shows whether it reached
year end, due rolls, unresolved deliveries due, missing snapshot members, and
members without a stored portrait. Unplayed years are not simulated. The
snapshot link opens the existing shared album; no duplicate image is created.

## A Sim’s branch journey

Choose any dynasty Sim in this section, or use **Branch journey** on their
profile. The timeline combines recorded founding/split checkpoints, births
whose branch is supported by membership, and reviewed transfers. It does not
guess undocumented moves or alter family relationships.
