Read Sims 3 saves automatically

Choose the game save that belongs to {{save.name}}. While the tracker is running, it checks every 10 seconds and reads a temporary copy after the game files have been unchanged for at least 30 seconds. Save normally in Sims 3 to send new information.

Supported: human Sims, names, sex, life stage, households and funds, saved family links, partner identity, traits, learned skills, and a positive pregnancy signal when present. Traits update existing Sim profiles and are included when reviewing new Sims in Automation Inbox; matching by game identity avoids duplicates.

Trait names: built-in traits use a bundled Sims 3 name dictionary, including saved hidden and reward traits. Unknown custom traits retain their full ID instead of a guessed name. Unreadable trait data does not erase previous traits. Save in the game to capture changes.

Saved clock, not live: the active world's day and time are read directly from the completed save. Turn on automatic day advancement below to follow future game midnights. The first reading keeps your current Global Day; a town change starts a fresh time alignment without losing challenge progress. Older clocks are rejected. This does not reveal exact birth/death times, illness, portraits, or delivery details.
