A NOTE FROM THE CREATOR

Why Sims 3 Clock Sync was removed

I couldn't figure out how to get Clock Sync working reliably for The Sims 3, so I've removed the Sims 3 Clock Sync download and setup options.

The desktop version reads completed Sims 3 save data as a supplement. It is not live Clock Sync and cannot detect everything that happens in game. Your existing Sims 3 tracker saves and records are still available.

What the desktop save reader can do

Read saved human Sims, households, available family links, traits, learned skills, positive pregnancy signals, and the active world's saved day and time. Newly discovered Sims appear in Automation Inbox for review.

With automatic saved-clock advancement enabled, future saved midnights advance Global Day. The first reading keeps your current challenge day. Nothing updates until you save in Sims 3; a browser alone cannot read the files on your computer.

Still manual: unsupported or missing information, exact birth/death times, illnesses, portraits, and delivery details. Review imported details and add or correct anything the save reader cannot confirm.

No Sims 3 relay or package required

You do not need to run the old Sims 3 batch files or relay. The tracker no longer starts that relay or accepts its reports.

If you previously installed the experimental SeveralUDO Sims 3 Clock Sync package, close Sims 3 before removing only that package from Mods/Packages. Do not remove other mods or your saves. The desktop reader never changes your game files.

Sims 4 Clock Sync is unchanged and remains available when using a Sims 4 tracker save.
