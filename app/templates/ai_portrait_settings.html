How to make a historical portrait

  1. Enable generation, choose a provider and save its setup.
  2. Open a Sim’s profile, then choose Generate historical portrait beneath a Tray or uploaded photo.
  3. Review the life stage, suggested year, region and style. Press Generate portrait.
  4. Find the finished image in Portrait Studio and on that Sim’s profile. The reference photo is never replaced.

AI aims for era-appropriate detail, but can make historical or identity mistakes. Review the finished portrait before treating it as part of your chronicle.
