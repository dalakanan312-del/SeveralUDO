How to play Infinite Decades
  1. Keep an in-game starting checkpoint and choose the first family line.
  2. Play that line continuously. Capture each departing branch at the moment it splits, keeping a matching in-game Save As file.
  3. Finish the line when it dies out or reaches modern day.
  4. Choose “Play newest unplayed branch.” Only the active branch's working date changes; the save and all Sim identities remain the same.
  5. Load the matching Sims checkpoint, reconnect its clock/save reader and confirm before importing reports.
  6. Repeat until all branches are complete. Export or duplicate the entire dynasty from Saves & Backups.

The tracker cannot pause households inside the Sims game or rewind game files. Set aging/story progression appropriately and keep separate in-game checkpoints. An uncaptured historical split cannot be reconstructed reliably after playing past it.
