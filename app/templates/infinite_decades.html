How to play Infinite Decades
  1. Choose Tracker calendar for one continuous Sims save, or separate game checkpoints if you prefer to restore in-game saves.
  2. Capture departing Sims as a new branch when they split. Their tracker dates and history are preserved.
  3. Under “Choose which branch to play,” pick a waiting or paused line. The tracker saves the current line and restores the selected line's Global Day.
  4. In Tracker calendar mode, keep the same game and clock connection. The first new report anchors the branch; later game days advance its tracker date. No game clock is rewound.
  5. In separate-checkpoints mode, load the selected Sims checkpoint, reconnect its clock/save reader and confirm before importing reports.
  6. Completed lines remain read-only. Export or duplicate the entire dynasty from Saves & Backups.

The tracker cannot pause households inside the Sims game, reverse their ages or rewind game files. Set aging/story progression appropriately. An uncaptured historical split cannot be reconstructed reliably after playing past it.
