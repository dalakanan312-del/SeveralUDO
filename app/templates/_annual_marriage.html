Young Adults are 18–39; Adults are 40–59. Elders use the Adult first-marriage roll and always use the after-five-years remarriage roll. At shared era boundaries, the newer era applies (for example, 1000 and 1450).

Widowed, divorced or abandoned Sims start in the following calendar year. The first five eligible years are years 1–5; an explicitly selected practical extension uses years 1–10. A refusal succeeds only on 1 on d6. Required arranged or political marriages override annual rolls.

Successful rolls suggest a date within that same calendar year. You choose the eligible spouse and play the wedding; the tracker does not change the game. Unresolved checks expire when the year changes without inventing results.
