Quick start

  1. Set your current day on Today, or create a private Game Clock link.
  2. Add or review Sims, households, pregnancies and relationships.
  3. Use the native die on Today. Results save immediately; any failed Sim roll automatically schedules death.
  4. Use Saves & Backups before major imports or edits.

What Archive means

Archive is a reversible hide: the record remains in backups and can be restored from its feature page. Permanent Sim deletion is reserved for accidental entries and cleans every dependent reference after showing an impact summary.
